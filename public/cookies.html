What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They enable the site to remember your actions and preferences over time.

The Yakuru mobile app does not use traditional web cookies; it stores preferences and session data locally on your device using secure platform-specific storage.

Types of Cookies We Use

Our website (yakuruhealth.com) uses four categories of cookies:

  • Strictly Necessary — login, checkout, security (cannot be disabled)
  • Functional — remembers your preferences (language, region)
  • Analytics — aggregated usage stats (privacy-preserving, no user identification)
  • Marketing — NONE — we do not use advertising cookies

Managing Your Preferences

You can manage cookie preferences anytime via the cookie banner on our website. You can also configure your browser to block all cookies or to alert you when cookies are being set.

Third-Party Cookies

We use privacy-first analytics from Plausible and Fathom that do not set third-party cookies or track users across websites.

Yakuru deliberately avoids tools like Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or advertising retargeting pixels.
